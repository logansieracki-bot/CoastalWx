    About this tool

    NorEASterCaster borrows the National Hurricane Center's presentation style — the cone graphic, the advisory stat line, key messages, and a plain-language discussion — and applies it to Nor'easters and other coastal lows, which don't get that same treatment anywhere else. Every number on this page right now is sample data for a fictional storm, wired through a single data object at the top of index.html so it's easy to replace with a real feed (NWS/NDFD, NOAA WPC surface analyses, or your own model output) — see the README in this repository for pointers on hooking that up.

    Not an official forecast. NorEASterCaster is an independent, unofficial hobby project. It is not affiliated with NOAA, the National Weather Service, or the National Hurricane Center. For official winter weather watches, warnings, and forecasts, always consult your local National Weather Service office at weather.gov. The NHC's own real hurricane forecasts, which inspired this page's layout, are at nhc.noaa.gov.
